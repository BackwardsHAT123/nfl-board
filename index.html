Play small, or pass: the only angle is 6-point teasers Spreads, totals and moneylines are priced efficiently, so the board passes on them. The one pattern that has paid in 27 seasons is moving underdogs of +1.5 to +2.5 up 6 points, which wins about 76% of the time against 73.9% needed. That edge is not statistically proven (about 1 standard error), so everything stays Low confidence.
